About This Platform

LoanIQ is an AI-powered loan eligibility prediction platform built to democratize access to financial clarity for Indian borrowers. The platform uses machine learning models trained on real-world lending data to predict home and education loan eligibility instantly — no bank visits, no waiting.

The integrated AI chat advisor runs on Ollama — a fully local, offline-capable LLM engine — meaning your financial data never leaves your device.
