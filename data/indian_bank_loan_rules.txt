INDIAN BANK LOAN ELIGIBILITY RULES (GENERAL)

1. Age Criteria:
   - Minimum Age: 21 years
   - Maximum Age at Loan Maturity: 60 years (Salaried), 65 years (Self-employed)

2. Income Criteria:
   - Minimum Monthly Income: ₹25,000 (Salaried)
   - Minimum Annual Income: ₹3,00,000 (Self-employed)
   - Stable income source required

3. Credit Score (CIBIL):
   - Minimum Required Score: 700
   - Preferred Score: 750+
   - No major loan defaults in last 24 months

4. Employment Criteria:
   - Salaried: Minimum 1 year in current job
   - Self-employed: Minimum 3 years in business
   - Valid income proof required

5. Loan Amount:
   - Personal Loan: Up to ₹25 Lakhs
   - Home Loan: Up to ₹5 Crores
   - Car Loan: Up to ₹50 Lakhs
   - Education Loan: Up to ₹1.5 Crores

6. Loan Tenure:
   - Personal Loan: Up to 5 years
   - Home Loan: Up to 30 years
   - Car Loan: Up to 7 years
   - Education Loan: Up to 15 years

7. EMI Rules:
   - EMI should not exceed 40% of monthly income
   - Total liabilities must be manageable

8. Documents Required:
   - Aadhaar Card
   - PAN Card
   - Address Proof
   - Salary Slips / IT Returns
   - Bank Statements (Last 6 months)

9. Interest Rates (Approximate):
   - Personal Loan: 10% – 15%
   - Home Loan: 7% – 9%
   - Car Loan: 8% – 11%
   - Education Loan: 7% – 10%

10. Processing Fee:
    - 0.5% – 2% of loan amount

11. Co-Applicant Rules:
    - Spouse / Parent allowed
    - Combined income considered

12. Property Rules (Home Loan):
    - Property must be approved by bank
    - Clear legal title required
    - No disputes on property

13. Prepayment & Foreclosure:
    - Some banks charge penalty
    - Floating rate loans often have no penalty

14. Rejection Reasons:
    - Low income
    - Low credit score
    - Job instability
    - High existing EMI
    - Incomplete documents

15. Special Categories:
    - Government employees get benefits
    - Defense personnel get concessions
    - Women applicants may get lower rates

Note:
These rules may vary slightly between banks such as SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, PNB, etc.
Always verify with official bank sources.
